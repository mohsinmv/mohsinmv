## 01 / Profile

I'm **Mohammad Mohsin**, a founder and hands-on product builder from Kerala, India.

I work across the complete business stack: **strategy, product, AI, software, design, growth, finance and operations**. My work is less about fitting into one technical title and more about turning real business problems into systems that can operate and scale.

```text
BUSINESS × PRODUCT × AI × ENGINEERING × DESIGN × GROWTH × OPERATIONS
```

> Technology is leverage. The product is the business system it enables.
