## About

I'm Mohammad Mohsin, a founder and hands-on builder from Kerala, India.

My work sits at the intersection of **business strategy, AI, product, marketing, operations, finance, and digital transformation**. I like taking messy real-world problems and turning them into clear systems, products, workflows, brands, and businesses.

I don't treat technology as the end product. I use it as leverage to build better companies.

- Building and operating multiple ventures across software, services, logistics, commerce, finance, education, and food
- Designing AI-first workflows and internal operating systems
- Building with modern web stacks, automation, APIs, databases, and AI agents
- Combining product thinking with marketing, finance, operations, and business strategy
