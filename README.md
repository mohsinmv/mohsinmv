## 01 / Builder, not one title.

I'm **Mohammad Mohsin**, a founder and hands-on builder from Kerala, India.

I work across **business strategy, AI, product, technology, marketing, finance and operations**. My strongest work happens where those disciplines overlap — taking an idea or messy real-world problem and turning it into a working company, product or operating system.

```text
BUSINESS × PRODUCT × AI × DESIGN × ENGINEERING × GROWTH × OPERATIONS
```

> I don't build technology for the sake of technology. I use it as leverage to build better businesses.
